Privacy policy

This website runs without tracking, without cookies and without analytics tools. In detail:

Controller

Thomas Breuss, Birsweg 5, 4203 Grellingen, Switzerland – email: me [at] tebe [dot] ch.

Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by cyon AG (https://www.cyon.ch/). When pages are retrieved, the hoster stores server logs (IP address, time, page requested) as needed for secure operation. See the hoster's privacy policy for details.

Stylesheet and fonts

The layout uses a stylesheet from this domain and the fonts already installed on your device. No stylesheets, fonts or scripts are loaded from third parties.

Login (only when using the admin area)

The login token lives exclusively in your browser (session storage) and is deleted when the tab closes. It reaches the server only as proof of login with your own requests. Failed login attempts are logged for a limited time as timestamps together with the IP address to protect against attacks – without usernames or passwords. No cookies are set.

Appearance

The chosen appearance (light/dark/automatic) is stored locally by your browser (local storage), in the admin panel and on this website. It never leaves your device either.

No sharing

No personal data is shared with, sold to or used for advertising by third parties.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification and erasure of your stored data, as well as the right to complain to the competent supervisory authority. Contact the address above for any of these.
