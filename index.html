Jaco CMS

The flat-file CMS that manages static HTML files directly in the web directory. No database, no build step – PHP 8.2 on any shared host. Edit pages inline on the live site, reorder content blocks by drag and drop, and keep automatic backups of every change.

Download Try locally

Why Jaco?

Inline editing

Edit pages on the live site, in place. Rich text, images, links and embedded videos – no separate backend screen.

Content blocks

Recurring sections that editors add, fill and reorder by drag and drop. No HTML knowledge required.

Automatic backups

Every overwrite or delete keeps a timestamped copy. Restoring is one click.

No database

The pages are the database. Everything lives in plain HTML files – portable, versionable, inspectable.

Four languages

The admin interface speaks English, German, French and Italian.

Free and open

Freeware, including commercial use. MIT-licensed source, no phone home, no licence validation.

Who is it for?

Jaco fits small static sites whose owners — not developers — keep the content up to date: restaurants and cafés with menus and opening hours, hairdressers with price lists, clubs with news and events, small companies and freelancers with a company site or portfolio, and private individuals with a family or hobby site. The pattern is always the same: the editable regions are marked once, then editors work directly on the page, no HTML knowledge required.

This site runs on Jaco

Every page you are reading right now is a plain HTML file in this repository, edited through the Jaco admin panel. The headings and paragraphs carry data-jaco-editable-line and data-jaco-editable markers – the same mechanism your own site will use.
